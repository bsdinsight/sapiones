Employee Certificates & Expiry Alerts

Keep every certificate, licence and qualification in one place — and never miss a renewal again.

A lightweight add-on for standard Odoo HR. Register the certificates and licences your people hold, and let Odoo watch the expiry dates for you — automatically raising a reminder for the manager before anything lapses. Ideal for safety, operations and compliance-driven teams.

📇 Certificate registry

One record per certificate: number, issuing body, issue & expiry dates, notes and an attached scan of the document.

🗂️ Certificate types

Configurable catalogue — safety, equipment operation, professional, language, degree… — each with a default validity period and a “mandatory” flag.

🚦 Automatic status

Every certificate is auto-classified as Valid, Expiring soon, Expired or No expiry, with a live “days to expiry” count.

⏰ Expiry reminders

A scheduled action creates a to-do activity for the responsible manager before a certificate expires — so renewals never slip through.

👤 On the employee form

A “Certificates” tab and an alert flag right on the employee, plus filtered list views of what is expiring or already expired.

🔓 Free & open source
